We compared dequantized FLOPA outputs with FP32 SDPA on the same captured Q/K/V.
At $N=4{,}096$, aggregating all 28 PixArt and 30 Wan self-attention layers at one
denoising step gives MAEs of 0.02793 and 0.01750 and RMSEs of 0.03689 and 0.02409,
respectively. These aggregates do not establish uniform per-head accuracy:
the worst relative L2 errors are 77.8\% for PixArt and 17.0\% for Wan.
Separately, four paired $1024\times1024$ generations gave a mean PSNR of
17.04~dB and SSIM of 0.648 against the original FP16 pipeline. The main subjects
were preserved, with visible differences in composition, texture, and reflections.
These limited paired-image comparisons quantify fidelity; they do not establish
near-lossless generation or population-level image quality.
